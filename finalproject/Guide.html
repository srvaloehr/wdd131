Tips

  • Keep a consistent running schedule
  • Focus on good running form
  • Use proper footwear

Recovery

  • Use foam rollers to ease muscle tension
  • Rest days are crucial
  • Maintain good sleep habits
  • Consider massages or physical therapy if needed
